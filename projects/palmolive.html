Context

Palmolive needed a cohesive, globally accessible digital presence for its product pages, built for scale.

For Palmolive, I was involved in the rollout of a scalable system for managing and presenting product information across international markets, using Adobe Experience Manager (AEM) with Product Information Management (PIM) capabilities.

Outcome

This project helped Palmolive create a cohesive, globally accessible digital presence, with product pages that are both user-friendly and easy to manage at scale.

  • Integrated AEM PIM for centralized control over product content across regional websites
  • Designed flexible templates supporting uniform structure with room for content variations
  • Coordinated multilingual content with accurate translations tailored to diverse markets

My contribution

  • Integration of AEM PIM for centralized product content management
  • Design and implementation of flexible, reusable templates
  • Coordination of multilingual content across international markets
