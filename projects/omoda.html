Context

Omoda & Jaecoo's Italian website needed regular content updates across its model, promotion and service pages.

Working in WordPress with the Gutenberg block editor, I implemented content across different page types on the Italian site — including model pages, active promotions, and service and warranty sections — keeping everything up to date with the brand's fast-moving offers and new model launches. Visual assets were pulled directly from Figma to match the intended design before being added to the pages.

Outcome

The result is a consistently up-to-date Italian website, with model, promotion and service content implemented and tested to render correctly across the site's block-based layout.

  • Implemented content for model, promotion and service pages using the Gutenberg block editor
  • Extracted and prepared visual assets from Figma designs for use on the site
  • Tested blocks and components to confirm correct rendering after content updates
  • Prepared and optimised visual assets in Photoshop to match brand guidelines
