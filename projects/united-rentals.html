Context

United Rentals' site is large and constantly evolving, spanning multiple markets and page-building systems.

This has been a several-month engagement that's still ongoing. The core of the work has involved building out pages for the US and Australian markets, modifying existing pages in both the legacy page editor and Drupal Paragraphs, and creating and adjusting custom blocks used across the site. Alongside that, a separate workstream focused on testing functionality for the new New Zealand market as it was rolled out.
