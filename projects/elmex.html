Context

Elmex needed consistent, localized product pages across multiple markets, built on AEM integrated with PIM.

This project focused on the creation and optimization of Elmex's product pages across multiple markets using Adobe Experience Manager (AEM) integrated with Product Information Management (PIM). The primary objective was to deliver consistent, informative, and localized content that offers a seamless user experience for customers worldwide.

Outcome

By leveraging AEM PIM and its full capabilities, this project successfully delivered a scalable and efficient solution for managing Elmex's diverse product portfolio online — ensuring both content integrity and a smooth, engaging experience for users across regions.

  • Implemented AEM PIM for centralized product data management and streamlined content updates across locales
  • Enabled efficient content localization for multiple languages and markets through comprehensive translation workflows
  • Delivered on-page SEO enhancements, improving organic visibility across markets
